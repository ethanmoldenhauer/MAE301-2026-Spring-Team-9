NestGPT

**Problem Statement**

The users for our product are people who are interested in pursuing living abroad in countries other than those which they were born in.
Oftentimes, when searching for housing options abroad, the different laws surrounding housing, as well as the processes regarding leasing and homebuying, are not very clear. On top of this, language barriers make it even more difficult to communicate with landlords and realtors, and visas and citizenship provide just another barrier to obtaining housing.

**Why Now?**

The number of people moving abroad for work, education, and remote employment has increased significantly in recent years. The rise of remote work has made it easier than ever for individuals to relocate internationally. However, the infrastructure for finding reliable housing information across countries has not kept pace with this mobility.
Recent developments in AI and large language models have unlocked the ability to use AI for deep, intricate research regarding complex subjects such as housing regulations and financial intricacies.

**Proposed AI-Powered Solution**

NestGPT is an AI-powered platform that compiles and explains housing information, legal requirements, and rental procedures for people moving abroad. The system will use AI to collect information from multiple sources and organize it into clear, accessible guidance for users.
On top of this, NestGPT can make decisions and create clear, personalized plans of action for the user on a situational basis. This gives easy access to support and answers to any questions that may arise during the relocation process, and it gives the user a holistic approach to their relocation process.

**Initial Technical Concept**

NestGPT’s initial technical concept is an AI system that combines structured housing and visa-related data with a GPT-style text model. The system would use data such as rent prices, cost-of-living estimates, housing rules, and visa requirements, along with text sources like rental procedures and landlord communication examples. A GPT-style model would support question answering, summarization, and translation, while a retrieval-based component would help ground responses in reliable country-specific information.

**Scope for MVP**

The minimum viable product (MVP) will focus on providing structured housing information for a small set of locations. Key features will include an AI chatbot for housing related questions, housing law summaries, up to date housing listings, and cost-of-living and rent estimates.
By limiting the amount of locations included, the development can focus on ensuring the quality and structure of the information before expanding the scope.

**Risks and Open Questions**

It may be challenging to verify whether NestGPT’s answers are fully accurate because housing rules vary by region and situation. Without clear ground-truth data, evaluating the system’s reliability can be difficult.
Housing laws and rental regulations can change frequently, this makes it difficult for the AI to keep information fully accurate and up to date.
People may hesitate to rely on an AI system for important housing decisions abroad. If users do not trust the information or find the system difficult, they may choose traditional sources instead.

**Planned Data Sources**

We plan to combine public housing and migration datasets, multilingual legal and translation corpora, third-party visa and translation APIs, and synthetic user-case generation to build and evaluate an AI assistant for people seeking housing abroad.
