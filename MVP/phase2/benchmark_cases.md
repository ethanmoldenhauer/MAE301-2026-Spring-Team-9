# NestGPT Benchmark Cases

Use these test cases to compare:

- `Baseline AI Mode`
- `NestGPT Research Mode`

Record what each mode gets right, what it misses, and whether the research mode gives more specific and current guidance.

## Case 1

- Country: Portugal
- City: Lisbon
- Budget: 1400
- Currency: EUR
- Reason: Work
- Timeline: 1 to 3 months
- Nationality: U.S. citizen
- Question: I want to rent before arriving. What documents do landlords usually ask for, what are likely upfront costs, and what scams should I watch out for?

## Case 2

- Country: Germany
- City: Berlin
- Budget: 1100
- Currency: EUR
- Reason: Study
- Timeline: 3 to 6 months
- Nationality: Indian citizen
- Question: What are the main difficulties international students face when trying to rent in Berlin, and is this budget realistic?

## Case 3

- Country: Japan
- City: Tokyo
- Budget: 180000
- Currency: JPY
- Reason: Remote work
- Timeline: 6 to 12 months
- Nationality: Canadian citizen
- Question: What should I know about guarantors, key money, and renting without strong Japanese skills?

## What To Capture

- Whether the answer mentions likely affordability
- Whether the answer includes local rental process details
- Whether the answer mentions visa or residency-related housing issues
- Whether the answer cites sources
- Whether the answer clearly states uncertainty and risks
